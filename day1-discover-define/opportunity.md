# Opportunity: Creating a Single Source of Truth tool for multiple policies sitting across multiple owners

**Scenario:** A · ServiceSG enquiry response

All quotes and metrics are synthetic and labelled as made up.

## Problem Framework

### 1. Goal

Improve the 1) Quality and 2) SLA response rate of replies

### 2. Problem

Customer service officers need to search multiple policy pages before replies, and these pages may not be accurate

### 3. Context

Customer service officers while they are on duty have to already focus on the content of tickets that they are serving. It is also not possible for officers to be completely trained on all policy changes and the context of when they were made.

### 4. Constraints

Customer service officers cannot keep up with all the policy changes

### 5. Success

The Single Source of Truth tool provides the right policy documentation and context, based on input from the customer service officer and the given context of the ticket

### 6. Explore

The SSOT tool helps train customer service officers on the job, making them more accurate and faster in their responses

## Evidence (synthetic)

### User quotes

- "I spend too long finding the policy pages."
- "There are too many policy changes and some queries are based on policies before the change was made."
- "I wish a tool can just recommend where to look."

### Metrics

- [Operations] Median first reply time
- [Quality] Agent Accuracy
- [Operations] Median resolution time

## Hypothesis

Officers will more quickly produce a first response if they can find policy pages more quickly

- Risk type: Feasibility (build a functional app first)
- Kill switch: The SSOT tool is unable to produce usable policy recommendations on first try
- Kill-switch signal: Customer service officers stop using the tool and depend on their personal links
- Success signal: 50% of all customer service officers in MVP scope use the tool at least on 20% of their ticket volumes, within 2 weeks
